4. Prompt Engineering & Structured Output
5. Context Management & Reliability

## Official Scenario Families

1. Customer Support Resolution Agent
2. Code Generation with Claude Code
3. Multi-Agent Research System

## Course Mapping

- Week 1 builds foundations for Domains 1, 2, and 5
- Week 2 focuses on Domain 3 with supporting Domain 2 and 5 concepts
- Week 3 focuses on Domain 4 with reliability spillover into Domain 5
- Week 4 applies all domains in scenario form

## Remediation Rule

When a learner misses a scenario judgment:

1. identify the weak domain
2. identify the scenario family
3. name the distractor or misconception
4. assign the smallest repair drill that fixes it
