# Testing System

This course uses four layers of testing.

## 1. Lesson Checks

Short checks after concept blocks.

- purpose: confirm immediate understanding
- format: quick scenario prompt
- source: `CHECKPOINTS.md`

## 2. Session Quizzes

Eight-question quizzes at the end of each session.

- purpose: test same-session retention and judgment
- pass target: 6/8
- format:
  - 2 warm-up questions
  - 4 scenario judgment questions
  - 2 anti-pattern questions

## 3. Competency Gates

Scenario-based mastery checks.

- purpose: verify skill transfer
- source: `COMPETENCY-CHECKS.md`
- rule: do not treat recall alone as mastery

## 4. Weekly Gates and Mock Exam

Larger readiness checks.

- weekly gates:
  - 12 questions
  - pass target: 75%
- final mock exam:
  - 24 questions
  - covers all 5 domains
  - covers all 6 official scenarios

## Readiness Rule

The learner is not ready just because they have seen the content.

They are closer to ready when they can:

- choose the best next action in a scenario
- explain why a tempting distractor is wrong
- connect the answer to an official `dX.Y` subdomain
- recover from weak areas through targeted review or labs
