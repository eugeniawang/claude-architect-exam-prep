The seven principles (the whole exam, compressed)

  1. P1 — Determinism for the correctness-critical; the model for judgment.
  2. P2 — Context is a finite, ordered, decaying working set — not memory.
  3. P3 — Match the mechanism to the problem's shape and cost.
  4. P4 — Make failure legible; propagate structured error context.
  5. P5 — Verification independent of generation; measurement disaggregated.
  6. P6 — Decomposition needs complete coverage and managed seams.
  7. P7 — Preserve provenance and uncertainty; never silently collapse conflict.

The Prime Directive: the LLM is one probabilistic component — architecture is everything you wrap around it. Wrong answers "ask the model harder" or build a cathedral. Spot that reflex and half the exam answers itself.
