# Practical Labs

These labs convert exam concepts into applied skill checks.

## When To Assign A Lab

- Session quiz below 6/8
- Weekly gate below 75%
- Right answer for wrong reason
- Mechanics-heavy topic where recall is not enough

## Lab Grading Rule

The learner passes a lab only if they can:

- choose the correct mechanism
- explain why one tempting alternative is wrong
- map the decision back to the official subdomain
